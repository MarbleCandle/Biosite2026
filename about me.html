About Me 

My passion for creation began behind a camera lens, deeply rooted in a childhood surrounded by photography. Inheriting this eye for capturing moments from my parents, I later expanded my visual storytelling into film production during middle school. Throughout high school, this passion grew into a national platform, allowing me to travel across the United States to teach others about filmmaking and share my experiences. This background has given me a deep love for creating and sharing compelling content, showing me firsthand how moving and still images can connect with an audience. 

Design Philosophy & Brand Storytelling 

What draws me to graphic and web design is the power of visual branding and advertising. I am fascinated by the idea that a single logo can hold an entire culture or tell a brand's story in a fraction of a second. To me, design is about taking someone's raw idea and giving it life whether that means crafting a distinct visual identity or translating a concept into a beautiful, functional website. I find immense fulfillment in creating specifically for people's needs, transforming abstract thoughts into tangible art that serves a clear purpose. 

Future Goals & Vision

As I build my digital footprint, my goal is to merge my background in photography and film with my love for graphic and web design to build cohesive, multi-media experiences. I want to partner with businesses and creators to elevate their digital identities through immersive web layouts and memorable visual assets. By combining my natural instinct for storytelling with structured design principles, I aim to create websites and campaigns that do not just look stunning, but genuinely resonate with people and leave a lasting impression.

Here is a list of some characteristics of me 

  • Multidisciplinary Creative: Proficient in translating abstract concepts into physical and digital realities through expert woodworking, graphic design, and web design.
  • Strategic Brand Designer: Fascinated by the intersection of marketing, culture, and typography, focusing on creating memorable logos that tell a deep story.
  • Pop-Culture Enthusiast: Deeply passionate about the complex world of Pokémon, studying its iconic brand design, massive global community, and evolving franchise culture.
  • Team-Oriented Collaborator: Highly effective at fostering communication, high-energy teamwork, and meaningful community bonds in both creative and athletic settings.
