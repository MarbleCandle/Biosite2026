Favorites things and hobbies

Welcome to a collection of my absolute favorite things, showcasing the hobbies and passions that spark my creativity. This page explores what inspires me most, from the incredible mechanical details of transforming robots to the precise art of hand-crafting furniture. Dive in to see how my love for intricate design connects everything I do!

 

Film and Photography

  • My Neighbor Totoro: My absolute favorite movie, which perfectly captures a sense of wonder through beautiful, classic visual storytelling.
  • Cinematography: The art of capturing mood and emotion through lighting, camera angles, and deliberate framing.
  • Camera Gear: Exploring the unique mechanics and lenses that bring a creative vision to life.

Mecha and Character Design

  • Michael Bay's Transformers: I am fascinated by the realistic, incredibly complex engineering and intricate visual designs of the bots in these films.
  • Slug: My top favorite character, standing out as an incredible powerhouse of both massive scale and mechanical detail.
  • Classic Art Styles and Totodile: I love the nostalgic hand-drawn aesthetics of older animation styles, alongside brilliant creature designs like my favorite Pokémon, Totodile.

Woodworking and Craftsmanship

  • Furniture Building: The rewarding process of transforming raw lumber into functional, beautiful pieces of art for everyday use.
  • Fine Sanding: Spending dedicated time smoothing out surfaces to perfect the shape and texture of a piece.
  • The Perfect Finish: Applying the final oils or stains to make the wood's natural grain and unique details pop.
